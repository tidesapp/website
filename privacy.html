Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rustam Sharshenov (“I”, “me”, or “the developer”) handles information in connection with the UK Tides Offline Android application (“the app”) and the website at tidesapp.co.uk (“the website”).

1. Information collected by the app

UK Tides Offline is an offline application. The app does not require an internet connection and does not connect to the internet for its core functionality. The app does not hold Android’s internet permission at all, so it is technically unable to make network connections.

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, telemetry, crash-reporting, or other data-collection SDKs.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

In particular, the app does not require or collect:

  • Names or email addresses.
  • User accounts or passwords.
  • Contacts, photos, or other personal files.
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising or tracking.
  • Analytics or telemetry data sent to the developer.
  • Usage data or browsing history.

The app stores your settings (chosen station, display preferences) as app data on your device; uninstalling the app deletes it. Tide predictions are calculated using information available locally on the device. No user data is sent to a server by the app.

Location (optional). To show distances and suggest the nearest station, the app may use your device’s location when it starts, if you have granted permission. It reads a single last-known location on your device — it does not track you, has no background-location permission, and cannot transmit it (the app holds no internet permission). You can decline; the app remains fully usable with a manually chosen station.

2. Feedback emails

The app includes a Send feedback feature. When you use this feature, your email application opens and prepares a message addressed to:

feedback@tidesapp.co.uk

The app does not automatically send the message. You decide whether to send it.

If you choose to send feedback, I may receive and process information contained in the email, including:

  • Your email address and other email header information.
  • Your name, if included in your email or email signature.
  • The content of your message.
  • Any attachments or other information you voluntarily provide.

I use this information to respond to feedback and support requests, investigate reported problems, and improve UK Tides Offline.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in feedback unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

Retention of feedback. I retain feedback emails only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, investigate issues, maintain appropriate records, and improve the app. When feedback is no longer needed for these purposes, I will delete it or securely dispose of it, unless I need to retain it to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.

3. Website and Google Analytics

The website at tidesapp.co.uk is hosted using GitHub Pages, a hosting service provided by GitHub, Inc. The website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to measure website traffic and performance.

Analytics consent. Google Analytics is not loaded when you first visit the website. Before Google Analytics is loaded, the website displays a consent notice allowing you to either Allow analytics or Don’t allow analytics. Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose Allow analytics.

If you choose Don’t allow, Google Analytics is not loaded and the website does not send Google Analytics data from your browser. Your choice is stored locally in your browser using localStorage so that the website can remember your preference. This choice is not sent to the developer or stored on a server by the website’s consent mechanism. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie preferences option on the website.

Information collected by Google Analytics. If you consent to Google Analytics, Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Information about visits and sessions.
  • Pages viewed and interactions with the website.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Approximate geographic location.
  • Pseudonymous identifiers used to distinguish users and sessions.

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies, including the _ga cookie. Google states that Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses from users in the UK, EU, or Switzerland. Instead, an IP address may be used to derive coarse geographic information, after which the IP address is discarded.

Google Analytics data is processed by Google for website measurement and reporting. Google’s own terms and privacy documentation apply to its processing of this information.

I do not use Google Analytics for advertising or sell website visitor information.

4. GitHub Pages

As the website’s host, GitHub, Inc. may process technical information about visitors as part of providing and securing the hosting service. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes. This processing is separate from Google Analytics and is performed by GitHub as part of providing the website hosting service.

For more information about GitHub’s privacy practices, please see GitHub’s privacy documentation.

5. Third-party services

The Android app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, telemetry, crash-reporting, or other data-collection services.

The website uses the following third-party services:

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Google Analytics — website analytics, loaded only after consent.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Where Google or GitHub process personal data outside the United Kingdom, they do so under appropriate transfer safeguards (standard contractual clauses with the UK addendum).
