2. What the App is

The App provides tide predictions for 45 stations around the United Kingdom. Predictions are calculated on your device from harmonic constants — a mathematical model of the main lunar and solar tidal constituents derived from historical observations at each station.

The App is an offline tool. It does not use a network connection, receives no real-time data, and applies no corrections for weather or current conditions. A tide prediction is an estimate of what the tide should be, not a measurement of what it actually is.

3. Accuracy and limitations

Harmonic predictions are a good first approximation, but the actual water level at a place can differ from the prediction — sometimes by a large margin — because many factors the model cannot account for affect the tide:

  • Storm surge. Strong winds, particularly in low-pressure systems, can push water onto the coast or draw it away — adding or removing well over a metre in parts of the UK.
  • Atmospheric pressure. Low pressure raises sea level by roughly 1 cm per hectopascal below normal; a deep low can add tens of centimetres, while a strong high can lower the level.
  • Perigean spring tides. When a spring tide coincides with the Moon at perigee (its closest approach to Earth), high water can be amplified well beyond normal.
  • Seasonal and long-term sea-level variation. Mean sea level varies with the season, with large-scale weather patterns such as El Niño/La Niña and the North Atlantic Oscillation, and with long-term sea level rise.
  • Local geography. Shallow water, estuaries, bays, headlands, and resonance can amplify, delay, or reshape the tide differently from place to place.
  • Station representativeness. A prediction refers to a specific station. The water level at your location — even a few hundred metres away — can be considerably different.
  • Local hydrology and works. River flows, heavy rainfall, snowmelt, dredging, silt build-up, breakwaters, and other coastal works all change local levels.
  • Inherent model error. Even in calm conditions, a harmonic prediction differs from actual observations at the station itself by some amount.

You should treat every prediction shown in the App as an approximate guide only — never as a measurement or a forecast of actual conditions.
